This policy explains how {{APP_NAME}} handles your information. {{APP_NAME}} is built to keep your data private: it does not collect, send or share any personal data.

How your data is stored

Everything you enter is stored locally on your device, in {{APP_NAME}}'s private app storage. Other apps cannot read it.

You can export your data to a file. The export only happens when you start it, and you choose where the file is saved or which app it is shared with. From then on, the file is handled by the place you saved or shared it, under that service's own terms.

When you import a file, {{APP_NAME}} reads only the file you select.

App lock with biometrics

If you turn on the app lock, {{APP_NAME}} uses your device's built-in fingerprint or face unlock. Your device's operating system checks your fingerprint or face. {{APP_NAME}} only learns whether the check succeeded and never has access to your biometric data.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled and shown by your device. They don't use a push service or any server.

Device backups

The backups are stored in your own account with that provider, under its terms. {{APP_NAME}} and its developer have no access to them.

Analytics, ads and third parties

{{APP_NAME}} contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking or services.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling {{APP_NAME}} deletes all data the app has stored on your device. Files you have exported yourself and device backups are not affected.
