BetBook

Privacy policy · Last updated 21 October 2026

At a glance

  • No personal data is collected or shared.
  • Your sites, deposits and withdrawals are stored only on your device.
  • The only internet use is optional exchange rates, and only currency codes are sent.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes your data.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking.

Overview

This policy explains how BetBook handles your information. BetBook helps you track deposits and withdrawals across betting sites. It is built to keep that information private: it does not collect or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. BetBook has no accounts and no servers. The sites, deposits, withdrawals and settings you enter are never sent to us or to anyone else.

How your data is stored

Everything you enter is stored locally on your device, in BetBook's private app storage. Other apps cannot read it.

Exchange rates

BetBook can update currency exchange rates so it can show your totals in one currency. This is the only time the app uses the internet, and it happens only if you tap to refresh the rates or turn on automatic updates. Automatic updates are off by default.

The rates come from Frankfurter, a free service that publishes the European Central Bank's reference rates. The request contains only the currency codes needed, such as EUR and DKK. No amounts, sites or other information about you are sent. Like any website, Frankfurter can see your device's IP address when it receives the request.

You can also enter exchange rates by hand and never use the online update.

Exporting and importing

You can export a backup of your data to a file. The export only happens when you start it, and you choose where the file is saved or which app it is shared with. From then on, the file is handled by the place you saved or shared it, under that service's own terms.

When you import a backup, BetBook reads only the file you select.

App lock

You can lock BetBook with a PIN, and also unlock it with your device's fingerprint or face unlock. The PIN is stored only on your device. Your device's operating system checks your fingerprint or face. BetBook only learns whether the check succeeded and never has access to your biometric data.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled and shown by your device. They don't use a push service or any server.

Device backups

If you have turned on backups on your device (for example Google backup on Android or iCloud backup on iPhone), your operating system may include BetBook's data in those backups. The backups are stored in your own account with that provider, under its terms. BetBook and its developer have no access to them.

Analytics, ads and third parties

BetBook contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only outside service it can contact is the exchange-rate service described above, and only if you use that feature.

Deleting your data

You can delete all your data from the app's settings. Uninstalling BetBook also deletes all data the app has stored on your device. Files you have exported yourself and device backups (see above) are not affected.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
