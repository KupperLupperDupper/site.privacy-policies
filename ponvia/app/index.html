Ponvia

Privacy policy · Last updated 21 October 2026

At a glance

  • No personal data is collected, sent or shared.
  • All data is stored locally on your device only.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes your data.
  • No analytics, ads or third-party services.

Overview

This policy explains how Ponvia handles your information. Ponvia is built to keep your data private: it does not collect, send or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. Ponvia has no accounts and no servers, and it does not use the internet. The weight entries, goals and settings you enter are never sent to us or to anyone else.

How your data is stored

Everything you enter is stored locally on your device, in Ponvia's private app storage. Other apps cannot read it.

Exporting and importing

You can export your data to a file. The export only happens when you start it, and you choose where the file is saved or which app it is shared with. From then on, the file is handled by the place you saved or shared it, under that service's own terms.

When you import a file, Ponvia reads only the file you select.

App lock with biometrics

If you turn on the app lock, Ponvia uses your device's built-in fingerprint or face unlock. Your device's operating system checks your fingerprint or face. Ponvia only learns whether the check succeeded and never has access to your biometric data.

Reminders

Weigh-in reminders are scheduled and shown by your device. They don't use a push service or any server.

Device backups

On Android, Ponvia's data is never included in Google backups and is not copied when you move to a new phone.

On iPhone, if you have turned on iCloud backup or back up to a computer, iOS may include Ponvia's data in those backups. The backups are stored in your own account or on your own computer. Ponvia and its developer have no access to them.

Analytics, ads and third parties

Ponvia contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking or services.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Ponvia deletes all data the app has stored on your device. Files you have exported yourself and device backups (see above) are not affected.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
