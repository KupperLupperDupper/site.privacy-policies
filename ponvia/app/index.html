Ponvia

Privacy policy · Last updated 22 October 2026

At a glance

  • No account, and no personal data is collected, sent or shared.
  • Your weights, goals and notes are stored only on your phone.
  • The app has no internet access at all.
  • Your data is not included in Google backups.
  • No analytics, ads or third-party services.

Overview

This policy explains how Ponvia handles your information. Ponvia runs entirely on your phone. There is no account and no server, and the app does not collect, send or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The weights, goals, notes, height and settings you enter are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Ponvia does not ask for permission to use the internet, so it cannot connect to any server, even by mistake.

How your data is stored

Everything you enter is stored locally on your phone, in Ponvia's private app storage. Other apps cannot read it.

Backups and new phones

Ponvia's data is not included in Google backups, and it isn't copied when you move to a new phone. To take your data with you, export it and import the file on your new phone.

Exporting and importing

The only way your data leaves your phone is an export you start yourself, in Settings › Data › Export. You choose where the file is saved or which app it is shared with. From then on, the file is handled by the place you saved or shared it, under that service's own terms.

When you import a file, Ponvia reads only the file you select.

App lock

You can lock Ponvia with a 4-digit PIN, and also unlock it with your phone's fingerprint or face unlock. The PIN is stored only on your phone, and there is no account to recover it with. Your phone's operating system checks your fingerprint or face. Ponvia only learns whether the check succeeded and never has access to your biometric data.

While the app lock is on, Ponvia blocks screenshots and hides its content in the list of recent apps.

Home screen widget

If you add Ponvia's widget to your home screen, it shows your latest weight there, where anyone who can see your screen can read it. While the app lock is on, the widget only says that Ponvia is locked.

Reminders and weekly summary

Weigh-in reminders and the weekly summary are scheduled and shown by your phone. They don't use a push service or any server.

The weekly summary is off unless you turn it on. Its notification shows your average weight for the week. Your phone's notification settings decide whether that text can be seen on the lock screen.

Analytics, ads and third parties

Ponvia contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party tracking or services.

When you open this policy from the app, it opens in your phone's browser. The browser loads the page; Ponvia itself still has no internet access. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub receives your IP address when the page loads, as with any website. The page sets no cookies and uses no tracking.

Deleting your data

You can delete all your data in Ponvia's settings. Uninstalling Ponvia also deletes all data the app has stored on your phone. Files you have exported yourself are not affected.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
